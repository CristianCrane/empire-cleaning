# Each Charge and refund is an attempt, recorded before the provider is asked

A Charge was asked for inside the transaction that recorded it, with a new random idempotency key each time. When the provider took the money but its reply was lost, or the transaction then rolled back, nothing recorded the Charge: the Job or Bill still looked owed, and the retry, under a new key, charged the Client again. The same held for a Deposit taken on acceptance (#241).

Now every payment off a saved card, and every Deposit, is a Charge attempt, committed in its own transaction before the provider is asked, with its own idempotency key. What the provider answered is written to it straight after. The next try for the same Job, Bill or Quote first asks after any attempt it never heard the end of, or that took money no Charge records, and only then works out what's owed. So what a lost reply took counts however the Job has changed since: it's given back once the Job owes less, and a weekly Bill bills only the rest (#534).

A next try comes from whichever happens first:

- **The Client or the Office.** For a Job, the Client taps "try again" on it, or the Office does; or the Client saves a new card, which tries each of their Unpaid Jobs and Bills. For a Quote, the Client or the Office accepts it again.
- **Something changes what's owed.** A completion corrected, an Adjustment answered, a cancellation, a plan changed or Ended, or a Bill voided settles its Jobs again.
- **The Clock, so nobody has to.** Every 10 minutes, open payments (`settleOpenPayments`) tries each attempt still open a quarter of an hour after it was asked, and each Job or Bill Unpaid only because the provider couldn't be reached. A Job is settled again and a due Bill charged again. Nothing else starts paying a Bill meanwhile (the one rule, below); one paid on its page from a form opened before gives back what its lost Charge took, and so does a Quote nobody accepted again. A Deposit waiting on the Client's bank longer than 15 minutes is called off, and the Client emailed, or, approved meanwhile, booked (ADR-0008, Bank approval). A declined card is left for the Client's new one. Lapsed credits' refunds are tried on every run already.

Each try holds the Job, Bill or Quote it's for, so tries from these at once take turns.

Within a day, while the provider still replays a key's first reply, it asks again with the same key and the same request, but only while that's what the try would ask now: as much, on the card the Client has now. One asked on a saved card the Client has since replaced, by saving another or by typing one in as they accept, would charge the old card; one for more than is owed now, as for a Job whose price came down or a Quote nobody accepted, would make a payment nobody wants, if the first never reached the provider (#534). Either is looked up by its key instead, which every payment carries in its metadata, as is any attempt after the day: a changed request takes a new key once the first is known not to have run and not to be running still ([Advanced error handling](https://docs.stripe.com/error-low-level#content-errors)). Not found within a quarter hour of when it was last sent (`sentAt`; the Clock may have sent it again since it was asked), it may still be on its way, so the Job, Bill or Quote waits on the provider, as after an outage; after that, it was never made, and what's owed now is asked under a new attempt. An attempt on a card typed in as the Client accepts names a token, used once, which can't be told from the next one, so only its amount counts. Money an attempt took is recorded as a Charge then, and counts towards what's owed; only the rest is asked for, and the rest declined leaves it Unpaid. An attempt declined is over, so the next try asks the card afresh.

We chose this over a key that's only stable across retries, which the provider forgets after a day and which replays a stored error. And over moving every call out of the caller's transaction, a bigger change that still needs a stable key for a lost reply.

## Consequences

- Taking money never throws for want of the provider: a lost reply, or an earlier attempt still unknown, comes back as "the payment provider couldn't be reached". The Job or Bill is Unpaid, and nothing more is asked for or given back until the attempt is known, by open payments if by nothing sooner. The Client accepting a Quote is told to try again in a few minutes.
- Every idempotency key is built in one place (`features/payments/keys.ts`), each kind of payment with its own shape, so no two share a key.
- A Deposit an earlier try took is the Deposit if it was for as much: the card it saved, if any, is asked of the provider when its reply was lost (#534). Otherwise it's given back, and a new one taken, but only once it's given back: one that can't be yet takes nothing more, for the Clock to give back, as two at once would be the Client charged twice.
- A payment still processing (no card does this off-session, but the provider allows it) isn't a decline: its attempt waits, and the next try asks how it ended.
- One rule: while a payment on something may still take the money, nothing else that moves money on it starts. A Bill's page offers no payment (#533), and Office Staff can't cancel it or record a payment made another way, as the screen says, until the Clock or a try has settled it. A Quote whose Deposit the Client's bank asks them to approve is being paid the same way (the attempt `approving`): Office Staff can't record acceptance or send changes to it, as its page says, until the Deposit is approved (and books it), refused, or called off, by the Client pressing Accept and pay again or by the Clock 15 minutes after it was asked (ADR-0008); one the Client's bank is already finishing then can't be called off, so it holds the Quote till the bank ends it, as the page says. The owner, 2026-11-23: "Yes, I think the one rule makes sense" (#534).
- Refunds are attempts the same way (`refund_attempts`): a refund the provider made but no Refund records is recorded by the next try for the same Job, Bill or credit, never given again. Neither table holds references, as each is written outside the transaction that holds what it's for.
- When Stripe may have done something after all (the connection failed, Stripe erred, or the same key's request is still in progress), or hasn't done it yet (a 429: over our rate, or the object locked by another request), the adapter throws rather than calling it a refusal, so the attempt waits to be asked about. Stripe processes no 429 and caches no reply for it, so the same key asks again ([Rate limits](https://docs.stripe.com/rate-limits); a rate limit read as a declined card, #534).
- Once a Job settles after a change to it, settling never undoes the change. If it throws, the error is reported and the Job is Unpaid until a retry settles it. One Bill that fails in the weekly run stops no other.
