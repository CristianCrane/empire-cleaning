# Site Visits are not Jobs

A Site Visit is a free visit by one Staff member to see a Property before it's quoted. It could have been a Job with a price of zero, which would have reused booking, the Schedule and Assignments. But Jobs come from accepted Quotes and carry Deposits, Charges, Assignments, completion, Adjustments and cancellation fees, none of which apply here, and each would need an exception. So a Site Visit is its own thing: Office Staff book it for one named Staff member with no Offer or Confirm, move or cancel it while telling the Client, and it ends Done, with its findings recorded on the Property, or Cancelled.

## Consequences

- The Schedule shows Site Visits alongside Jobs.
- A Site Visit by a Cleaner with Available Hours that day takes room like a Job with a Crew Size of one, its length as its Labour Hours, and is refused where there's none. One by Office Staff alone takes no Cleaner time and is only kept from overlapping that person's other Site Visits.
- Its findings update the Property's price-affecting details, which only Office Staff correct.
