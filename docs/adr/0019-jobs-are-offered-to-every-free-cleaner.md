# Jobs are offered to every free Cleaner

Office Staff used to Offer each place on a Job to a named Cleaner, and that Offer held the place until it was Confirmed or Released, so every Job waited on someone in the Office. Now a Job's places that nobody holds are its Open Places, and once the Job is within 7 days of starting, each one is open to every Cleaner who is free for it: they have Available Hours that day and hold no Job or Site Visit whose time overlaps it. The first to Confirm takes the place. Only a Confirmation or a named Offer from Office Staff holds a place; Office Staff can still make one at any time, for a Client's regular Cleaner or anyone else. Office Staff step in only when a Job stays Unassigned.

We chose this over Cleaners volunteering and Office Staff picking from them, which keeps the Office as the bottleneck. Open Places are worked out when a Cleaner looks, not stored as an Offer to each free Cleaner when they open, so someone who becomes free later sees them, and a filled place disappears from everyone's list with nothing to withdraw. Offers go out 7 days ahead, not when a Job is booked (up to 28 days ahead for a Service Plan), because who is free is only reliable nearer the day, once holidays and changed hours are set. Fairness caps or rotation were left out: Office Staff can balance work with named Offers until it proves a problem.

## Consequences

- Confirming checks again that the place is still open and the Cleaner still free, and is refused otherwise.
- A Cleaner can say "Not for me" to an Open Place; it leaves their list and they're never emailed about it again.
- A Confirmed place that is Released opens to every free Cleaner but the one who Released it; the 48-hour Release cutoff is unchanged.
- When a Job moves, its crew are Offered the new time and keep their places, except anyone not free then, whose places open.
- Before Confirming, a Cleaner sees an Open Place's Service, time, time on site, Crew Size and area (town and postcode), never its street address, Access Instructions, Client or prices.
- Cleaners get one email at 7am in New York, only on days when a Job is newly open to them, listing all their Open Places, new ones first; an Open Place on a Job starting within 48 hours is emailed at once.
- A Job nobody Confirms stays Unassigned and appears in Needs attention from 48 hours before, as before.
- Once Staff have skills per Service (#123), only those with the Job's Service are free for it.
