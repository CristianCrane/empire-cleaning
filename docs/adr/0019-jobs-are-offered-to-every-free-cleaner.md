# Jobs are offered to every free Cleaner

Office Staff used to Offer each Job to named Cleaners, one for each of its Crew Size, and that Offer kept them on its crew until they Confirmed or Released it, so every Job waited on someone in the Office. Now each Cleaner a Job still needs, beyond those on its crew, is a Cleaner Needed, and once the Job is within 7 days of starting, it is open to every Cleaner who is free for it: they have Available Hours that day and are on no Job or Site Visit whose time overlaps it. The first to Confirm is on the crew. Only a Confirmation or a named Offer from Office Staff puts someone on a crew; Office Staff can still make one at any time, for a Client's regular Cleaner or anyone else. Office Staff step in only when a Job stays Unassigned.

We chose this over Cleaners volunteering and Office Staff picking from them, which keeps the Office as the bottleneck. Cleaners Needed are worked out when a Cleaner looks, not stored as an Offer to each free Cleaner when they open, so someone who becomes free later sees them, and a Job with its whole crew disappears from everyone's list with nothing to withdraw. Offers go out 7 days ahead, not when a Job is booked (up to 28 days ahead for a Service Plan), because who is free is only reliable nearer the day, once holidays and changed hours are set. Fairness caps or rotation were left out: Office Staff can balance work with named Offers until it proves a problem.

## Consequences

- Confirming checks again that the Job still needs a Cleaner and the Cleaner is still free, and is refused otherwise. It also names the time the Cleaner was shown, and is refused if the Job has moved since (#242).
- Office Staff can Offer a Job only to a Cleaner free for it, so every Offer can be Confirmed (#242).
- A Cleaner can say "Not for me" to a Cleaner Needed; it leaves their list and they're never emailed about it again.
- A Confirmed Assignment that is Released leaves a Cleaner Needed, open to every free Cleaner but the one who Released it; the 48-hour Release cutoff is unchanged. Office Staff taking someone off a Job Releases it for them, so it isn't open to them either, though the Office can still Offer it to them (#242).
- A Cleaner deactivated, or whose day goes to 0 hours, has their Assignments there Released and opened to the others (#158, #242).
- When a Job moves, its crew are Offered the new time and stay on it, except anyone not free then, who leave a Cleaner Needed.
- Before Confirming, a Cleaner sees a Cleaner Needed's Service, time, time on site, Crew Size and area (town and postcode), never its street address, Access Instructions, Client or prices.
- Cleaners get one email at 7am in New York, only on days when a Job is newly open to them, listing all their Cleaners Needed, new ones first; a Cleaner Needed on a Job starting within 48 hours is emailed at once.
- A Job nobody Confirms stays Unassigned and appears in Needs attention from 48 hours before, as before.
- Once Staff have skills per Service (#123), only those with the Job's Service are free for it.
- The word was "Open Place" until #148: a place on a crew read oddly to the owner, and the Office screens already said "Needs 1 more Cleaner". Code, tables, screens and emails all say Cleaner Needed, and a Cleaner is "on the crew", never "holding a place".
