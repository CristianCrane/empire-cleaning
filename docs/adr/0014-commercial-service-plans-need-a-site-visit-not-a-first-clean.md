# Commercial Service Plans need a Site Visit, not a First Clean

A residential Service Plan can only start after a Completed First Clean, a trial for both sides that also lets Empire Cleaning check the home is as described. For business premises, the required Site Visit already does that checking before any Quote is Sent, and a business agreeing to cleans 5 or 6 days a week won't book a one-off trial first. So a commercial Property can be offered a Service Plan Quote once a Site Visit has recorded its findings, with no First Clean. Residential Properties keep the First Clean rule.

## Consequences

- No Quote for a commercial Property can be Sent before a Site Visit has recorded its Premises Type and floor area. A Draft can be written at any time.
- For premises Office Staff already know, such as existing commercial Clients brought into the system, they record the findings as already done, without booking a visit.
