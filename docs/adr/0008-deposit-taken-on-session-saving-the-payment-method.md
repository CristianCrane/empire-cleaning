# The Deposit is taken on-session, by the payment that saves the Payment Method

When a Client accepts a Quote, the Deposit is an on-session payment, made while they are on the page, and that same payment saves their card as their Payment Method for later Charges. Every later Charge (the Balance on completion, acceptance Office Staff record on a Client's behalf, late-cancellation and No Access fees) is off-session, on the saved card. With Stripe, the browser's Payment Element hands our server a one-time token, and the server creates and confirms the Deposit's PaymentIntent from it, saving the card by the same payment. We chose this, Stripe's documented way to "charge a deposit and save the card for the full amount later", over saving the card first and charging the Deposit off-session at once. That two-step way is the less reliable path while the Client is present: banks are more likely to want approval that nobody is there to give.

## Consequences

- **Bank approval.** A Client's bank may want them to approve the Deposit (3D Secure). Then nothing is booked yet: the browser runs the approval, and the server finishes the booking when the payment has succeeded. The provider's webhook finishes it too, in case the browser never comes back. Whichever is first books it. Meanwhile nothing else moves money on the Quote (ADR-0022's one rule). The Client pressing Accept and pay again before approving calls the first payment off with the provider before a second is asked, so they're never charged twice; one approved meanwhile books the Quote instead, and one still processing is waited out. One nobody approves within 15 minutes (`APPROVAL_MS`) is called off by the Clock, and the Client is emailed, as they may have meant to approve it later in their bank's app (#534).
- **Atomicity across approval.** The booking stays atomic (ADR-0001), though approval happens outside the transaction. Once approved, booking checks again that the Quote is still Sent and its day still has room. If not, the Deposit is refunded and nothing is booked.
- **Cards only.** Payments are limited to cards, which succeed or fail at once. Link is left out too, since it also offers bank payments. This deliberately departs from Stripe's advice to let it choose payment methods dynamically: bank debits and other delayed methods settle days later, which would break "the Deposit succeeds before anything is booked".
- **A later Charge the bank wants approved.** Nobody is there to approve an off-session Charge, so one the Client's bank wants them to approve takes nothing: the Job or Bill is Unpaid for them to approve, and they're emailed to approve it on its page, where Approve payment confirms the same payment on their saved card with them there, as Stripe advises for an off-session payment that needs authentication ([Save and reuse](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/save-and-reuse#charge-saved-payment-method)). Acceptance Office Staff record on such a card books nothing: the Client accepts on their quote's page, where their bank asks them (#534 Q7).
- **Consent.** Saving a card is the Client's consent to later off-session Charges. The payment forms say what those are, and we record when each Client agreed.
- **Saving without paying.** A card saved without paying (where the Office's add-a-card email leads) is set up on its own for off-session use.
- **The Payment Gateway interface** (ADR-0004) gains "take a Deposit, saving the card it was paid with", "how did a Deposit that needed approval end", and provider events in our own terms. Stripe's objects still don't pass it.
