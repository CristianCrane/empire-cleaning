# Adjustments are priced from the Price List, reviewed by the Office and approved by the Client in the portal

Cleaners never put a price on anything. When they mark a Job Completed they report what was different on the day, from a short list: an Add-on done that wasn't on the Quote, an Add-on on the Quote that wasn't done, or extra time on site. Each report is priced from the Price List version the Job's Quote was priced with (the Add-on's hours, or the extra Labour Hours, at its hourly rate, taxed at its one-off rate unless the Add-on is tax-exempt), so changing the Price List never reprices a booked Job. That priced report is the Adjustment.

A reduction (a quoted Add-on not done) counts at once, at the Quote's own price for it: nobody's approval is needed to charge a Client less. An increase goes to Office Staff first, who propose it to the Client, at the worked-out price or one they override with a reason, or dismiss it. The Client then approves or declines it in the portal; one not answered within 7 days lapses, after one reminder. The Balance doesn't wait for any of this. It is charged at completion, on the quoted price with reductions, and an increase the Client approves is charged afterwards as the rest of the Balance, with its own receipt.

We chose this over the Cleaner entering amounts (pricing is the Office's job, and should follow the business's rates, not one person's judgement on site), and over the Client approving on the Cleaner's phone, whether by the Cleaner ticking that they agreed or by the Client typing their name. Both leave only weak evidence that the Client agreed to pay more, and the Client is better placed to decide in their own time with the price in front of them.

## Consequences

- "Increases need the Client's approval on site" becomes approval in the portal after the Job. Unapproved increases (declined, lapsed, dismissed, or still waiting) leave the Job at its quoted price.
- A Job's Balance can be charged in parts: at completion, then once for each increase approved. A failure of any of them makes the Job Unpaid, until a retry (by the Client, by the Office, or by the Client saving a new Payment Method) takes what's owed.
- What's owed is always worked out afresh: the quoted price, plus every Adjustment that counts, less every Charge and refund so far. Completing, approving, correcting and undoing a completion all just settle the difference, charging or refunding it.
- Office Staff correct a completion by changing or removing Adjustments (an approved increase can only be lowered: the Client agreed to that much), correcting the reported Condition, or undoing the completion altogether, which refunds everything but the Deposit and puts the Job back to booked.
