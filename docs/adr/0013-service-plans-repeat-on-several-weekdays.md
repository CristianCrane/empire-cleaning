# A Service Plan can repeat on several weekdays, and its fees follow each Job's price

Service Plans first repeated on one weekday, every 1, 2 or 4 weeks. Empire Cleaning's commercial Clients have agreements for 5 or 6 days a week, so one plan can now repeat every week on several weekdays, each with its own time and visit (Clean Type, Labour Hours, Crew Size and price), since a Saturday clean often differs from the weekday ones. We chose this over one plan per weekday, which would multiply Deposits, Quotes, On Hold and Ending by six for what the Client sees as one agreement, and over one shared visit for every day, which breaks as soon as one day differs. A plan on one weekday still repeats every 1, 2 or 4 weeks; one on several repeats every week.

The plan's single Deposit and its fees were defined against one Job's price. With six Jobs a week, a fee of "the plan's Deposit amount" would charge a late cancellation of one evening's clean a deposit sized to the week. So the plan's Deposit is the Deposit percentage of one Job on each of its weekdays, and a plan Job's Late Cancellation or No Access fee is the Deposit percentage of that Job's own price. For a plan on one weekday, both come out exactly as before.

## Consequences

- A Service Plan Quote lists each weekday's visit and price, then the total for one round and the Deposit. The Client accepts the pattern whole; for fewer days, the Office sends a new Quote.
- Accepting needs room for the first Job on each weekday. Later Jobs whose day has no room wait for the Office to book or skip them, as before.
- Skipping, On Hold, changing terms and Ending work per Job and per plan as before. The Deposit still goes to the plan's final Job when it Ends.
- Each Job is still charged on completion. Batching a commercial Client's charges into a weekly or monthly bill, or invoicing them, is a separate decision.
