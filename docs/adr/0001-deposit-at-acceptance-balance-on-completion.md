# Deposit at acceptance, Balance on completion

Accepting a Quote charges a Deposit (a standard percentage, overridable per Quote) to the Client's saved Payment Method, and the Quote only becomes Accepted, and the Job only booked, if that Deposit succeeds. The Balance is charged automatically when the assigned Staff mark the Job Completed. We chose this over full prepayment (awkward for recurring Service Plans and for on-site Adjustments), invoicing after the Job (Empire Cleaning carries the collection risk), and charging only after completion (no protection against late cancellations). The Deposit also doubles as the Late Cancellation and No Access fee, so one mechanism covers both commitment and penalties.

## Consequences

- A Service Plan holds a single Deposit for its whole life rather than one per Job; plan Jobs charge their full price on completion, and the held Deposit is applied to the final Job when the plan Ends.
- An on-time cancellation, or any cancellation by Empire Cleaning, refunds the Deposit in full.
- A reduction Adjustment can push the Balance negative, in which case the difference is refunded.
