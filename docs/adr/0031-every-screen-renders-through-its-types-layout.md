# Every screen renders through the layout for its type

Each screen built its own page from the design system's parts: its breadcrumbs, its title row, the subtitle under it, where its actions sit, its facts grid and its bar at the foot. So screens of one kind came out differently each time: a Record's actions in a menu on one screen and loose on the next, five places the breadcrumbs start, 25 facts grids by hand in three styles (#426, `docs/screens.md`). The owner reported 18 such cases on six screens (#424), and fixing them a screen at a time left the cause in place. This retires that pattern: **a screen building its own page from the kit's parts**.

Every screen is one of seven types, which the owner agreed from the map on 2026-11-13: Record, List, Home, Form page, Settings, Message page and Calendar (`docs/ux.md`, Screen types). Each type has a layout in the design system (`packages/ui/src/layouts/`) that places the page's parts, and a screen renders through the one for its type, filling its slots. A new screen takes a type; one that fits none is a new type, settled as `docs/ux.md` says before it's built.

A lint rule holds it (`empire/screen-layout`, `packages/lint`): a screen that places a page part itself is flagged, and the kit, where the layouts live, is outside it. It's a warning while the layouts are built one type at a time (#432 to #436), its count the measure of what's left, and an error after (#437). We chose this over a page-header component screens could use or skip, which would have left each screen deciding its own page again, and over fixing the reported screens one by one.
